Key Takeaways and Next Steps

Scale success. Nurture opportunity

Views continue to build success of top funnel metrics, but frequency drives down funnel conversions. Aim to drive multiple exposures per candidate to drive interest and increase applies.

Handshake Strategy

Start with paid campaign for the successful schools to continue momentum and smaller incremental tests for the schools presenting more of an opportunity to grow. We can provide a comprehensive plan that outlines forecasted performance for the next hiring cycle.

Build on Down Funnel Momentum

Metro, Greenfield, Lakeshore and Pine Valley have above average hire rates and make up around 73% of all hires last year. Continue building on that success by scaling campaigns to reach more of the total population. Lakeshore is also a battleground with both tech and bank peers having a significant branding budget.

Handshake Strategy

Pine Valley and Greenfield have smaller markets, so let's scale those AND find look alike schools that will perform similarly.

Target Skills for a more direct approach

It's important to dive into the skill granularity. You may see that certain majors and interests garner a stronger candidate pool leading to more hires. For schools that have the scale but aren't getting the performance you want, let's look into actual skills and interests.

Handshake Strategy

Handshake can provide 1st party skills data so Deacon can hyper target their down funnel strategy for relevant applies leading to more hires and a shorter candidate cycle. These are not inferred skills but manually chosen and proven by individual candidates.
